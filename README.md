# B-J-Biscuit-Commercial-Performance-and-Executive-BI-Dashboard
BandJ Biscuit required a comprehensive business analytics solution to evaluate revenue distribution, customer demographics, geographic performance, and profitability drivers across 10 distribution areas.
The objective was to transform raw transaction data into interactive executive dashboards to identify high-value customer segments, optimize sales strategies, and address employee performance.
Tools and Methodology
Analytics and BI Tools: Power BI and Excel for data modeling, dynamic measures, and executive dashboard development.
Data Transformation and Modeling: Aggregated transaction records, engineered price tier logic (Low ≥ $10, High < $10), and calculated key KPIs including Profit Margin (%), Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), and demographic distributions.
Data Visualization: Designed three dedicated analytical reporting views—Sales Performance, Customer Demography, and Sales Representative Performance—equipped with multi-variable interactive filters.
Key Findings and Analytical Insights
High Revenue and Healthy Margins: Generated $61.57M in Total Revenue across 3.05M units sold, delivering $26.78M in Total Profit at an exceptionally strong 43.50% overall Profit Margin (COGS: $34.78M).
High-Tier Product Dominance: Low-priced biscuit categories generated over $50M of the $61.57M total revenue. Shortbread emerged as the single most profitable biscuit brand, followed by Vanilla Wafers and Ginger Snaps.
Core Demographic Target: Male buyers accounted for 83.89% of total gender revenue ($20M+ generated specifically from Male Seniors). Seniors and Middle-Aged demographics represent the company's core buyer profile.
Geographic and Individual Drivers: San Antonio ranked as the #1 performing location by revenue, profit, and overall customer base, with top buyer Robert Hernandez contributing 40,472 units ($845,382 in revenue).
Sales Rep Performance: Travis Doyle emerged as the top-performing sales representative by total revenue and percentage profit contribution.
Payment Distribution and Seasonality: Payment methods are evenly split across Mobile Payment (25.16%), Debit Card (25.13%), Cash (24.88%), and Credit Card (24.83%). Monthly profit remained steady at ~$3M–$3.5M from January through July before experiencing a steep decline starting in August through the rest of the year.

Strategic Recommendations
Seasonal Intervention: Investigate the August revenue drop to address supply chain, inventory, or seasonal marketing bottlenecks before Q3.
High-Performing Product Bundling: Create promotional product bundles pairing top-tier sellers (Shortbread, Vanilla Wafers) with lower-performing brands (Butter Crunch, Lemon Crisps) to drive volume across the entire product line.
Demographic and Regional Targeting: Reallocate digital marketing spend toward male senior and middle-aged demographics in top-tier geographic hubs like San Antonio, San Jose, and Philadelphia.
Sales Incentive Programs: Replicate the territory management and client outreach practices of top sales reps (Travis Doyle, Joseph Hill) across lower-performing sales teams.
